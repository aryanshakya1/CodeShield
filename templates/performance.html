Cryptographic Observation: SHA-256 is an ultra-fast symmetric hashing algorithm, whereas RSA private-key signing is significantly more compute-intensive due to large modular exponentiation with the private exponent d. RSA public key verification is typically faster than signing because public exponent e = 65537 has very few 1-bits (Hamming weight of 2).
